TripPlanner {{ app_version }} is a class-project demonstration with six curated destinations: Paris, Lagos, London, New York City, Tokyo, and Cape Town.

How your ideas are chosen

We compare your chosen interests, pace, and allowance with the local place collection. Each match has a reason. A simple scheduler spreads eligible ideas across your days without repeating places. This is rule-based personalization; it does not use a trained language model or make bookings.

About this free demo

The website runs on Render Free and uses a separate Neon Free database. After 15 minutes without traffic the website may sleep; the next visit can take about a minute to wake it. The database also sleeps when inactive. Monthly usage limits or provider issues can temporarily interrupt access. Saved trips are kept in the database across ordinary website restarts. No continuous-availability guarantee is provided.

Your online collection

Saved trips are stored on this website's server. A protected browser cookie separates your collection from other visitors. The cookie expires after 365 days without a visit. Clearing cookies, private browsing, or changing browser/profile/device can lose access. There is no account recovery or cross-device sync. People sharing this browser profile share access.

Unreachable saved trips are not automatically deleted when a cookie expires. The service operator can access stored data for operation and backups. Avoid sensitive information. Current drafts use a signed browser-session cookie; signed does not mean encrypted. Appearance is remembered in this browser.

There are no ads, analytics, GPS collection, payments, or automatic uploads from the downloadable apps. Using this online planner sends the preferences you submit to the server. The host receives ordinary connection information such as your IP address and may keep operational logs under its own policy.

Before you travel

Costs and time slots are illustrative. Check current prices, opening hours, travel times, accessibility, dietary details, and suitability yourself. Flights, accommodation, transport, and unscheduled meals are not included. This is a useful starting point, with honest gaps where our small collection cannot fill your day.
