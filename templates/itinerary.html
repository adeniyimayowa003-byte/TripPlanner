Small rules. A clearer plan.

How this itinerary was built

  1. Start with your ranked matches. The existing interest scores, matching preference tags, and cost tie-breaks decide which eligible ideas come first.
  2. Follow your pace and simple categories. Relaxed means Morning and Evening; other supported paces have Afternoon too. Fill mornings across all days, then afternoons if included, then evenings. Attractions and activities go in daytime; evenings prefer restaurants, then use a fitting attraction or activity as a fallback, not a meal.
  3. Keep ideas unique and costs together. Never repeat a place. Choose the first unused matching idea that fits the day's exact equal share; skip one that is too costly and consider it on later days.
  4. Leave honest gaps. This greedy approach does not optimize a route or budget. Daytime choices can use allowance a restaurant would need. No opening-hours, duration, or travel-time check is performed, and meals are not fully covered, even when every slot has a suggestion.
