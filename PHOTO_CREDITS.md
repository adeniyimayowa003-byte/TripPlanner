# TripPlanner photo credits

These locally bundled photographs retain their individual licenses. No local image edits were made. The interface uses cropped display frames; the bundled JPEG files remain unedited. Preserve the credit, source, license, and change note when redistributing a photo, including in native app bundles. License/source/photographer links need internet; these credits and image files are bundled locally.

CC BY-SA images retain the listed license; image derivatives must comply with its share-alike terms. The photograph licenses do not automatically apply to the rest of this application.
