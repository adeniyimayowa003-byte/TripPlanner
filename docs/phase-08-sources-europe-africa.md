# Reviewed place references — Paris, London, and Cape Town

Reviewed on **2026-11-01** for the local TripPlanner class-project catalog. The prepared 24 records are in `.tmp/release-europe-africa.json` for the project owner to integrate after the test gates. This research did not change the live catalog.

Each city has eight distinct places, two named food venues, at least four meaningful interest tags, and free/public and paid choices. All seven original Paris IDs and names are retained. The added Paris record is Musée d'Orsay.

## Estimates and evidence

Every monetary value is an **illustrative per-person planning estimate, not a live price**. A positive number is not a quote for a specific ticket, menu, visitor category, or booking. A zero applies only to the documented free general-entry or public-walk activity; purchases, food, temporary exhibitions, sports facilities, tours, and other optional activities can cost extra. Sources should be checked before travel.

Descriptions are short original paraphrases of factual venue/official-tourism information. Addresses identify the actual venue or a clearly described starting point. No coordinates or Place IDs were guessed. No claim is made that itinerary order is a walking route or that an address alone verifies current access.

## Reuse and verification limits

Only short original descriptions and factual place/address references were prepared. No source HTML, maps, reviews, menus, logos, or photographs were downloaded or copied into the app. A source link is not a blanket redistribution license. Image files require their own source/creator/license verification before bundling; these references do not grant photo rights.

Date-valid provenance, a safe HTTPS link, and schema validation cannot independently prove that a real-world venue remains open or that an estimate matches a future bill. The user interface must retain its illustrative-estimate and external-source explanations. Real-spot Google Maps opening still needs final manual verification by the project owner.
